Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026 · Applies to the Heedmark app for Android

The short version. Heedmark has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your library, journal, prayers, and listening positions stay on your phone and in a file in a folder you choose. We don't collect, sell, or share your personal information, and the app has no ads or analytics.

What Heedmark stores

To work, the app keeps the following information. All of it is created by you or read from files you point the app to.

  • Library details: the location, title, author, and chapter lengths of audiobooks in the folder you select, plus your listening position in each book.
  • Journal entries: your text, tags, and the book and timestamp an entry is linked to, including saved highlights.
  • Prayer requests: your text, reminder frequency, updates, and status (active, answered, discontinued).
  • Settings: things like the sleep timer and library sort order.

This information is stored on your device. It is not sent to the developer or to any server operated by the developer.

Your data file

Heedmark can save a copy of the information above to a file named heedmark_data.json (and a backup copy) in the audiobooks folder you choose, so it survives reinstalling the app and can be moved to another phone. This file stays in your folder. It is plain text and not encrypted, so keep that folder private. If you place the folder inside a cloud-sync service (for example a cloud drive), that service will handle the file under its own terms.

Permissions the app asks for

PermissionWhy
Access to your audio files / the folder you pickTo find and play your audiobooks, and to save the data file in the folder you grant. Access is through Android's system folder picker.
NotificationsTo show playback controls on the lock screen and notification shade.
Foreground service (media playback) and wake lockTo keep audio playing when the screen is off.
Biometric / device authenticationOnly if you turn on the Journal lock. Heedmark asks Android to verify you. Your fingerprint, face data, or PIN is never seen or stored by the app.
InternetUsed only to download the app's fonts (see below).

Network use and third parties

Heedmark does not upload your content anywhere. The one network activity is downloading its typefaces (Playfair Display and Inter) from Google Fonts. When that happens, Google receives standard connection information such as your IP address, handled under Google's Privacy Policy. No journal, prayer, or listening data is included in those requests.

This website loads the same fonts from Google Fonts. It does not use cookies, analytics, or ad trackers.

Sharing

When you choose to share a snippet clip or text, Heedmark hands it to Android's share sheet and the app you pick receives it. Heedmark does not see or control what happens next, and nothing is shared unless you tap Share.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Edit or delete any journal entry, prayer, or highlight inside the app.
  • "Clear library" in Settings removes the audiobook list (not your audio files, journal, or prayers).
  • To remove everything, uninstall the app and delete heedmark_data.json and heedmark_data.backup.json from your data folder.
  • The Journal lock can be turned on or off in Settings, and is stored per device.

Children

Heedmark is not directed to children under 6 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, since it collects none.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, for example by adding encryption or any online feature, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
