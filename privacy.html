Privacy policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Heedmark is a devotional audiobook companion. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

In brief: Heedmark is designed to keep your library, journal entries, prayer requests, and listening progress on your device. The app does not require you to create an account.

Information you choose to store

When you use Heedmark, you may create journal entries, prayer requests, and saved audio snippets, and the app records listening progress and library details. These may include the text you enter, dates, audiobook titles, and playback timestamps. Based on the app's current design, this information is stored locally on your device using its app storage.

Audio files and folder access

You choose which device folder Heedmark can use to find your audiobooks. The app uses that access to list and play supported audio files. Your audiobook files remain where you stored them; Heedmark does not upload them to a Heedmark server.

Sharing

If you choose to share an audio snippet or other content, Heedmark passes the item to your device's sharing feature. The content is then handled by the app or service you select, under that provider's own privacy practices. Sharing is initiated by you.

Information collected by the developer

The current app design described for this release does not require an account or a Heedmark-hosted service to store your journal, prayers, library, or playback position. This policy does not make claims about information that your operating system, app store, or a third-party app may collect independently.

Notifications and device permissions

Heedmark may request access needed to select and read audio files, play audio in the background, and display playback controls or notifications. You can manage app permissions through your device settings. If notification or reminder features are enabled in a future version, their behavior will be described here.

Data retention and deletion

Your locally stored app data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the relevant data within the app, or uninstall Heedmark. Clearing the audiobook library is intended to remove library entries, not your journal or prayer entries. Device backups, if enabled, are governed by your operating system and backup provider.

Children's privacy

Heedmark is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated account or server.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Heedmark's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when changes are made. Please review this page periodically.
