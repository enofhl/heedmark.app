How it works

One quiet gesture: mark the moment.

A line stops you. A passage speaks to you. Heedmark remembers exactly where it was, so you can come back to it, write about it, or pray over it.

Listen

Pick the folder where your audiobooks live. Every book appears on your shelf, chapters in the right order, with lock-screen controls.

Mark

Save a highlight, start a journal entry, or tag a prayer request. Each one is linked to the book and the exact second.

Reflect

Return to your journal and tap play to hear the passage you wrote about. Pray through your list day by day.

Features

Built for people who listen.

For scripture, devotionals, and other spiritual audiobooks you already have as audio files.

🎧

Your library, your folder

Choose one parent folder; books and their section subfolders are found automatically.

⏱️

A player made for devotion

Seamless chapter transitions, skip 10 seconds, speeds up to 3×, a sleep timer, and a gentle 15-second rewind whenever you pause.

📓

Journal with audio links

Entries remember the book and timestamp. Tap play to jump back and hear what you were hearing.

🙏

Prayer list with rhythm

Daily, weekly, or monthly reminders; a Today tab; updates on each request; Answered and Discontinued lists.

✂️

Highlights and snippets

Mark a start and end, save it as a highlight, replay it, or share the audio clip itself.

🔒

Private journal lock

Optionally protect the Journal with your fingerprint, face, or phone PIN. Heedmark stores no password of its own.

📁

Portable data

Your library, positions, journal, and prayers are saved to a file in your own folder, so they survive reinstalling and move to a new phone.

🌙

Light and dark

A warm, quiet design with a dark mode that’s easy on the eyes at bedtime.

🕊️

No pressure

No streak guilt, no pushy notifications, no feed. Just you and what you're hearing.

Get the app

Join the alpha test.

Heedmark is in alpha testing on Google Play for Android. It takes about two minutes: join the tester group, opt in, then install.

  1. 1

    Join the Google Group

    Open the Heedmark alpha test group and tap Join group. Sign in with the same Google account you use in the Play Store on your Android phone.

    Open the Google Group
  2. 2

    Become a tester

    Once you’re in the group, open the tester sign-up page on your phone and tap Become a tester. Use the same Google account again.

  3. 3

    Install Heedmark

    On that same page, tap the link to Heedmark on Google Play, then tap Install. Updates arrive through Google Play like any other app.

Page not available, or no Install button? Check that you joined the group with the same Google account your phone uses in the Play Store. It can take a few minutes after joining before the sign-up page works.
